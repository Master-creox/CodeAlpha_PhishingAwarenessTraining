## What the deck covers

1. Cover — Phishing Awareness Training
2. Agenda — what the training covers, learning outcomes
3. What is phishing?
4. How a phishing attack works (4-step flow)
5. How to spot a phishing email
6. Fake websites — how to check before you trust a link
7. Social engineering tactics
8. Real-world phishing patterns
9. Best practices — verify, OTP rule, MFA, password manager, updates + reporting
10. Key takeaways
11. Interactive quiz (3 questions)
12. References — CISA, APWG, NIST, CERT-In, National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal
13. Closing — "Think before you click"

Every slide carries the footer "Phishing Awareness Training — CodeAlpha Internship, Task 2" with its page number.

## How to view it

Open `codealpha-phishing-awareness-training.pptx` in PowerPoint, LibreOffice Impress, or Google Slides (File → Import). No audio or narration is included — the deck is meant to be presented or read as slides.
